## Decided

| Item | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Treads | 5/4 hardwood, 1" finished, 10½ wide; stringers dropped 1" | Rev T's stair geometry implied zero-thickness treads |
| Stringer top | Each stringer extends **9" under the landing** (y 18→27) with a top-run cut at 48.96 (landing 49.71 − ¾ ply) | The real plumb cut against a rim at y 27 is only 7.0" tall (Rev T's "11¼ bearing / 15¼ deep" counted a phantom). | Landing box | All **2×8** at z 41.71→48.96: rim (y 16½→18, x 107→129½), side member (y 1½→16½), right-wall ledger (y 0→18), bed-wall ledger (x 107→129½). Two 2×4 joists at x 114¾ and 121¾ running with the stringers. 2×4 blocking y 25½→27 between top runs | One elevation, every hanger fully backed by the header (41.5→50.75), everything above the 41.5 panel top |
| Rim ends | **HUC28** concealed-flange hangers both ends — header side with SD screws through the ¾ facing | An LUS flange would foul the side member on the left and hang past the ledger end on the right |
| Rim ↔ side member | 2 × A35 at the inside corner | Side member butts the rim's back face |
| Stringer A | 4 × ¼×4½ SDS into the header through the facing over its top run; 2 toe-screws to the rim | Can't be screwed from inside the box — the side member occupies x 107→108½ |
| Stringer B | 3 × ¼×3½ SDS from inside the box through the rim into its end, before the deck | Full 7¼ bearing does the work |
| Stringer C | Plumb cut on the ledger's end (y 18); 4 × ¼×4½ SDS into right-wall studs; 2 toe-screws to the ledger end | The ledger, lagged to studs, is the only solid thing at that wall |
| Rim width | Stays 22½ (butts the right-wall ledger) — **builder's full-width proposal declined** | A full-width rim takes its right end off the lagged ledger and onto an unlocated stud |
| Nook opening | Finished with ¾ poplar jambs on trimmers and header, matching the wrap. **Finished 42½ × 40¾.** Flat soffit panel (¾) face at 40¾, flush through the opening; top at 41½, 0.21 under the 2×8s. | Rake gussets | Deleted | The stringers now reach the flat ceiling |

## Parked (open findings carried into Rev U)

- **Beam left end**: side ledger stops at y 48, beam is y 47→50 — 1" of 3" backed. Recommended fix: ledger to 50¾ + double-2×10 hanger (or a cleat). Also fixes slat 0. Builder chose to leave for now.
- **Room-depth chain**: door 150→182 + 3½ = 185½ vs 186 room — ½" unplaced; field-measure.
- Everything in `T-connection-checker.md` §2 not yet declared: deck-ply front-edge blocking at the beam, header-to-post straps, kicker anchorage, beam lamination, slat base fastening, floor-joist line loads under the half-wall and kicker.
- Screen top plate y-position (47¼→50¾ text vs 46½→50 + ¾ band in Drawing 3) — decide before the ceiling-joist measurement.

## Framing order (landing)

1. Right-wall ledger (y 0→18) and bed-wall ledger (x 107→129½) lagged to studs.
2. Side member to the header: 6 × ¼×6 SDS through the facing.
3. HUC28s on the header face and the ledger face at y 16½→18; set the rim.
4. A35 ×2 at the side-member/rim corner.
5. LUS24s; set the two joists.
6. Stringers A, C, then B (B's screws from inside the box). Kicker.
7. Blocking at y 25½→27. Deck ply, treads, risers.
