Sheet nesting — 9 sheets

Every panel placed by a guillotine first-fit-decreasing nest that reserves 1/8 on every cut, drawn here at true size on 48 × 96 sheets. No two pieces overlap and none runs off a sheet — both are asserted when the takeoff is built, so this is a layout you can mark out rather than an indicative count. ⟳ marks a piece the nest turned; rotation is allowed everywhere because 3/4 ply is inside its span rating in either direction at this joist spacing, so an appearance call can override one. No factory-edge trim is allowed for: the first piece sits in the corner, so two usable edges are assumed. Click any sheet to enlarge it; ←/→ step through all 9 of them.
