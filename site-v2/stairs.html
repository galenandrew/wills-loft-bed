4 — Section through stringer B

Annotation

Cut through stringer B. Each stringer runs 9 on under the landing; its top plumb cut at y 18 is 7.01 tall, close to the 2×8 rim it bears on. Stringers are dropped ¾ for the ¾ plywood treads. The 6 stair risers are 8¼; the last step onto the deck is 8½, because it is the framing stack over the nook header rather than a stair riser — do not step 8¼ off 7 times. The half wall is in front of this plane and draws dashed. Switch the Loft on to see where the deck lands relative to the landing. The nook light sits in the flat part of that ceiling at 41, with 3¾ of chase above the panel before the landing joists — a canless wafer, not a can.

6 — Tread & riser joint — step 3 of 5

Components
Layers
Annotation

The joint everybody builds from memory and gets wrong. The exposed face of every plumb notch is its downhill side, so the riser board goes in front of the cut with its bottom edge on the stringer's horizontal cut for the tread below; the tread above starts where that riser ends and butts its face. A simple butt joint, riser behind the tread, nothing notched — and the nosing-to-nosing going stays exactly 9.
