Annotation

Cut at x 55, in the bay between joists, so the ply, ledger, beam and blocking are hatched and the joist beside them reads pale. The wrapped beam's top is 2¼ proud of the mattress, so it retains it rather than sitting under it, and the boxed ledge's lid tracks it at 3 proud — the curb on the bed-wall side. Sitting headroom is 30 over the mattress.

9 — Loft framing plan

Components
Layers
Annotation

Joists hang off the rear ledger and off the beam's inside face — LUS24s both ends; they cannot land on a beam that shares their bottom. Nothing bears in the field. Joist 0 runs on past the beam face to y 50, sistered to the ledger, and the beam is notched over the pair; sheet 10 is that joint. Two things follow: the ledger wants a stud within 6 of y 50, and the deck ply must be glued and screwed onto the ledger, because that diaphragm is what stops it rolling. Switch Finish on to lay the deck ply back down.

10 — Plan cut at z 56 — inside the notch

Components
Layers
Annotation

The beam is one 2×14 LVL notched 3 × 3½ out of its bottom left corner, so its end sits on the 2×4 side ledger and on joist 0 sistered beside it — 5.25 sq in of seat, about 115 psi against 425 allowable. A 2×4 ledger cannot back the beam's end on its own, which is why the joint is a notch and a sistered joist rather than a butt into a hanger. Field item: the window wall wants a stud (or added blocking) within about 6 of y 50 — the reaction lands 1½–3 off the wall face and a 3½-deep ledger has a short couple to resist it.

Annotation

The ledge is a box, not a shelf: a ¾ ply lid on 2×2 cleats, a ply front rail, and the deck ply as its floor. The lid stands 3 proud of the mattress — the curb on the bed-wall side — and runs the full 107, over the stair face at the deck end. The two outlets are flush in the lid, one at each end; their boxes hang into the well, clear of both 2×2 cleats and of every strut. Wire them before the lid goes down — afterwards the well is a closed box.
