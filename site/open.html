MEASURE — 4 left

Field measurements still owed. Anything depending on one is provisional.

  • Stud locations — bed wall, window wall, right wall.Every ledger fixing and stringer C depend on them. The window wall needs a stud (or added blocking) within ~6″ of y 50 for the beam's end reaction.
  • Ceiling joist nearest 50½ from the bed wall.Decides whether the screen top plate screws to a joist or wants blocking first. Does not gate the design — the plate is fixed and the attic is accessible.
  • Floor joists under the half-wall bottom plates and under the kicker.
  • Room depth: door casing to the closet-wall corner, and the overall 186.The 4″ to the corner is assumed, not measured. Nothing built depends on it — the ½ lands in the stair-to-dresser gap either way.

SPECIFY — 6 left

Chosen by the builder; the model carries a placeholder until then.

  • Kicker anchorage to the floor.The weakest chain in the model — the stair's whole horizontal thrust runs stringers → notch hooked over the kicker → kicker → floor. Declared and UNSPECIFIED since Rev AY. Needs the floor joists located first.
  • Whether to toe-screw the stringers to the kicker.The three stringer → kicker rows are bearing with no fastener at all, so the hook is currently the only thing resisting thrust.
  • Bottom-plate anchorage to the floor — hw_bottom_plate_a / _b.Two boards, y 0–3½ and 46½–50, each running the full 28¼ from the half wall through the nook.
  • Framing species and grade.
  • Slat stock — match screen_top_plate (3½ × 1½ poplar 8/4 S4S or clear/select pine).The 23 slats and the plate are one assembly under one colour; different species there is two woods under one paint.
  • Ply grade for the two paint-grade pools (¾ and ½).One pool per thickness, both paint-grade — 4 sheets of ¾ + 5 of ½. Buying the deck as rated sheathing would break the ¾ nest, which assumes a single pool: reopen that deliberately or not at all.

NOW-OR-NEVER — 10 left

Each of these gets much harder, or impossible, after the step that follows it.

  • Nook power into the half-wall — before sheathing.Switch on the half-wall face, desk side.
  • Loft ceiling light wiring — before loft_ceiling.The ½ ply screws straight to the joist bottoms; there is no chase to fish through afterward.
  • Confirm all three loft-light positions — before the ceiling ply is cut.Positions and switch location are ASSUMED. Both ceiling seams run on joist centres and clear all three fixtures, but the sheet is cut once.
  • Shelf blocking between the nk_bedwall_stud bays — before nk_wrap_bedwall.Only if the nook shelves might be heavy or high. That sheet stays ¾ ply (0–41½, the tall wall) so it takes a screw anywhere; ½ would not.
  • deck_blocking — before beam_wrap_face.Screwed from the beam's OUTBOARD face at y 50, so the wrap cannot close that face first. Same trip as the wrap's own structural fixing over x 0–27.
  • deck_seam_blocking — before the deck ply.Toe-screwed into the joists at both ends; there is no face to drive it from, and it is unreachable once the ply is down.
  • Set out and fix both bottom plates as one board each — before the jamb packs.
  • Nook lining — before the nook face.The face laps the ends of all three lining sheets.
  • Stringer B's screws — before the landing deck.
  • Risers — before the treads. Stair-side skirt — last of all.Each tread butts the face of the riser behind it. The skirt lands on top of the landing box and the stringers, and the treads and risers butt it.

VERIFY — 2 left

Cut once you have checked, not before.

  • Lay out and cut one stringer, measure the real throat and plumb cut, then cut the other two.
  • Do not step 8¼ off seven times — that lands at 57¾, ¼ short of the deck. The seventh riser is 8½.

Closed — 7 decisions

DECIDED Open only to check that something is not being reopened by accident
  • Header-to-king straps: scrapped (Rev AS). The half wall is sheathed on BOTH faces across the joint — hw_sheath_stair z 24–58 and hw_sheath_loft_head z 15.57–53¾ both lap the header at z 41½–50¾ and run onto both kings at y 0–1½ and 48½–50 — and two continuous top plates bear over the header and both kings. Nailed to header and kings that is a stiffer tie than a strap. Rev AZ took the loft face of that sheathing ¾ → ½; accepted without a calculation, on the grounds that the joint carries gravity into the trimmers, whose bearing is checked, so the sheathing here is a racking and continuity gusset rather than a tension tie. Rev BA.1 removed the strap markers Drawing 7 was still showing eight revs later.
  • Slat-to-plate joint: specified. Adhesive + one #8 × 2½ angled into the plate per slat; 16ga pins for assembly only. The screen is the guard for the bed, so the top is a structural joint, not trim.
  • Screen top-plate position: y 47–50½ (Rev AS). Attic access means blocking can be added wherever the joist lands.
  • Ledger fixings: ¼ × 4 (right wall) and ⅜ × 4 (bed wall), sized off 1½ of ledger + ⅝ of board.
  • Deck-ply front-edge blocking added (Rev AU): nine 2×4 blocks on edge at the beam face — eight at 10½ on a 12 pitch plus one 5¾ at the rim, one 8 ft board. It closes the last gap in the diaphragm; the joists hang off the beam, so the deck had no fastening to it at all.
  • Mattress measured 2026-09-06: 8 mattress + 2 topper = 10. Sitting headroom is 28, not 32.
  • Ply pools settled (Rev AZ): about 93 sq ft of painted face moved ¾ → ½, so the buy is 4 sheets of ¾ + 5 of ½ where it was 9 + 1 — one sheet fewer, five of them thinner and cheaper. Both pools paint-grade: the deck joined the ¾ pool at Rev AV (it is the finished walking surface) and the two structural jamb flitches plus the header's middle ply share the ½ pool.
