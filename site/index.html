Conventions

  • Every drawing is generated from dimensions.yaml. Nothing here was placed by hand. If a drawing looks wrong, the data is wrong — fix the yaml, run python3 verify.py, then python3 build.py.
  • Orientation. Plans: bed wall at the top, window wall to the left. x–z views look toward the bed wall (window wall left). y–z views keep the bed wall on the left — sections cut at an x position are drawn mirrored where needed so that stays true.
  • Hatched = cut by the view. Pale = beyond the cut. Dashed = hidden. Blue = hardware. Red = a limit or an open finding.
  • Framing vs finished. Member extents are framing (actual lumber). Platform 50½ finished / 50 framed. Half-wall 4¾ finished where it shows / 3½ of studs — and 4 where the stair runs against it. Deck 107 / 106¼ rim. Nook 43 × 41½ rough / 41¾ × 41 finished. Cut lists use the framing numbers.
