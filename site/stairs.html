4 — Stair section

Cut through stringer B. Each stringer runs 9 under the landing: its top plumb cut, at y 18, is 7.01 tall — close to the 2×8 rim it bears on. Stringers are dropped ¾ for the ¾ plywood treads — no nosing, just a 1/8 overhang with a tape-sealed edge, painted. Tread/riser joint: the riser board goes on the front of each plumb cut, its bottom edge on the stringer’s horizontal cut for the tread below; that tread starts where the riser ends and butts its face. Simple butt joint, riser behind the tread, nothing notched — every board is a full 24 wide and the nosing-to-nosing going is exactly 9. At the foot the stringers are notched 1½ × 3½ over the kicker. All seven risers are 8.286; the last is the sideways step from the landing onto the deck. Half-wall framing (dashed) sits in front of this section from this angle. Handrail on the right wall for the full run.

5 — Under-stair nook

Rev X. The nook is one ¾-plywood-wrapped tube running from the half-wall's outer face at x 102 straight through to the right wall — no jambs, no separate head trim. Its ceiling is flat at 40.96 to y = 18.27, where the stringer undersides come down to that line, then rides them to 14.51 at the far end; the framing plane above it is 41.71, the bottom of both the nook header and the landing ledgers, so the panel screws straight to them with nothing to fur. Inside the wall the raked run is carried by hw_rake_nailer, a 2×4 laid flat with its underside on the stringer line, bevelled where it dies into the header. The two sides are nailer walls: 2×4 studs on the flat into 2×2 plates, in line with the trimmers, so the reveal and the nook faces are one plane. No bottom plate crosses the opening — the floor runs through. Power for the wafer light must be in the half-wall before it is sheathed.

8 — Stair & landing framing

Section at y 20 cuts the three top runs: A is fixed by SDS into the header through the facing, B by SDS from inside the box through the rim, C bears on the ledger end and screws to the right-wall studs. The landing box is four 2×8s at one elevation (41.71→48.96) — rim, side member, bed-wall ledger, right-wall ledger — with two 2×4 joists running with the stringers and 2×4 blocking flush with riser 6. The rim hangs on concealed-flange HUC28s: into the header (SD screws through the ¾ facing) and into the right-wall ledger. Framing order: ledgers → side member (6 × ¼×6 SDS through the facing) → HUC28s and rim → 2 × A35 at the side-member/rim corner → LUS24s and joists → stringer A (4 × ¼×4½ SDS into the header over its top run, 2 toe-screws to the rim), stringer C (4 × ¼×4½ SDS into right-wall studs, 2 toe-screws to the ledger end), then stringer B (3 × ¼×3½ SDS from inside the box through the rim) → kicker → blocking → deck ply, treads, risers.
