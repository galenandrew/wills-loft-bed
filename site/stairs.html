4 — Stair section

Cut through stringer B. Each stringer runs 9 under the landing: its top plumb cut, at y 18, is 7.26 tall — exactly the 2×8 rim it bears on. Stringers are dropped 1 for the 5/4 treads. All seven risers are 8.286; the last is the sideways step from the landing onto the deck. Handrail on the right wall for the full run.

5 — Under-stair nook

The opening through the half-wall is finished with ¾ poplar jambs; the head jamb and the flat soffit panel share one plane at 40¾, so the ceiling runs straight through the opening. The panel's top at 41½ clears every 2×8 in the landing box by 0.21. Where the stringer undersides come down to that line (y ≈ 18.23) the panel simply continues on the stringers to 14.26 at the far end. No gussets. Power for the wafer light must be in the half-wall before it is sheathed.

8 — Stair & landing framing

Section at y 20 cuts the three top runs: A is fixed by SDS into the header through the facing, B by SDS from inside the box through the rim, C bears on the ledger end and screws to the right-wall studs. The landing box is four 2×8s at one elevation (41.71→48.96) — rim, side member, bed-wall ledger, right-wall ledger — with two 2×4 joists running with the stringers and 2×4 blocking flush with riser 6. The rim hangs on concealed-flange HUC28s: into the header (SD screws through the ¾ facing) and into the right-wall ledger. Framing order: ledgers → side member (6 × ¼×6 SDS through the facing) → HUC28s and rim → 2 × A35 at the side-member/rim corner → LUS24s and joists → stringer A (4 × ¼×4½ SDS into the header over its top run, 2 toe-screws to the rim), stringer C (4 × ¼×4½ SDS into right-wall studs, 2 toe-screws to the ledger end), then stringer B (3 × ¼×3½ SDS from inside the box through the rim) → kicker → blocking → deck ply, treads, risers.
